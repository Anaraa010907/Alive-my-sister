# Audio notes — ch2 «Чонын хөндий» (`js/audio-ext.js`)
- All procedural: each sound is one `Voice` (osc/noise → filters → envelope → `A.master`, with an optional send to `A.echoIn`). Every node is disconnected once its last source ends. One-shots are timed on `AC.currentTime` with no timers. Peaks are `vol`×(≤0.25).
- Measured peaks at vol 1 after the 0.9 master (worst of 4 random renders): growl .15, howl .13, step .03–.09, bark .18, caw .15, flap .05, breath .16, shriek .14, crawl .14, bones .14, grass .14, heartbeat .16. Music: chase .19, valley .04 (≈.085 when a distant howl sounds).
- `TW.music.chase|valley.start(A[, vol])` / `.stop(A)` are idempotent, so calling them every frame is fine. A start during a fade-out revives the same instance, so on/off toggling never stacks layers. Fades take 1 s. The setInterval look-ahead scheduler (100 ms tick, 1 s ahead) clears itself once the fade ends, or when the AudioContext closes or changes.
- chase: a 128 bpm low kick (accents 1 & 3 plus pickups), quiet 8th-note ticks, a deep boom every 4 bars, and a drone of 55 Hz against 58.3 Hz (a minor 2nd) plus a 77.8 Hz tritone. valley: brown-noise wind with a moaning band-passed tone, plus far, dark howls every 11–24 s through the echo bus (35% of the time a second wolf answers). It sits under the engine's own wind.
- Suggested engine use: `wolfStep` per footfall (about every 0.17 s when chasing), `wolfGrowl` on alert, `wolfHowl` in the howl state, `ravenCaw` + `ravenFlap` on alarm, `shulamBreath` every 3–5 s when idle, `shulamCrawl` while it hunts or moves, `shulamShriek` on attack, `bones` per sister step on bones, `grassRustle` on entering or leaving grass, `heartbeat` (about 1.1 s loop) while hidden with a wolf near.
- Preview: open `audio-preview.html` (a button per sound, combos, a vol slider, music toggles, the prototype wind bed). Test: `python3 tests/audio/test_audio.py` (OfflineAudioContext renders, checks peak/RMS/NaN, fade-out, interval cleanup and start/stop spam).
