# Art notes — js/art-enemies.js (preview: art-preview.html, ?scale=1|3, ?t=sec)
- **Style:** pure silhouettes in `P.sil` with lines 0.5–3 units, at the prototype's scale. Each creature is drawn twice: first widened in `P.rim`, then filled with `P.sil` on top. That leaves a thin outer rim that keeps ink-black shapes readable on dark hills and the dead sky. The only lit marks are eyes (`P.eye`).
- **Wolf:** lean steppe wolf with withers at 21, deep chest, tucked waist, long legs drawn with IK, bushy tail and cheek ruff, glowing almond eye. Poses: `patrol` lateral walk (head below the back line, tail low); `sniff` nose to the ground, tail low; `alert` frozen, ears up, stiff tail, raised hackles; `chase` flying gallop (spine stretches and gathers, ears flat, mouth open); `howl` sitting, muzzle raised, jaw trembles; `lost` diagonal trot, head down. Unknown states use the patrol pose.
- **Raven:** heavy hooked beak, shaggy throat, wedge tail; idle bob, glances, head dips and tail flicks. `look` shows the head front-on (beak toward the camera), so it works whenever `e.face` flips. `alarm`: wings half spread and flapping, beak opening on a caw rhythm, two faint caw arcs. `cooldown`: feathers ruffled, head tucked, a short shake when it starts.
- **Shulam:** eyeless, sunken profile; four long thin limbs with elbows and knees above the body (spider-like), long fingers, visible spine, stringy hair hanging over the face, faint ribs, mouth slit. `listen` raises the head, freezes it and shows faint rings closing in on it. `hunt` is a fast skitter with jitter and hair streaming back. `search` sweeps the head. `attack` lunges with arms reaching about 24 units forward, using `e.t`.
- **Timing used:** `e.t` (seconds in the current state) eases `alert` in (0.14 s), `howl` (0.4 s) and `attack` (0.22 s); `e.anim` drives every gait (one gait cycle = 2π). For enemies.js, no foot slide: advance `e.anim` by distance × k. Wolf patrol k≈0.35, lost (trot) k≈0.24, chase (gallop) k≈0.16; shulam idle/return k≈0.45, hunt k≈0.22. A single k≈0.3 also looks fine.
- **Grass:** the back layer is a dense clump 33–44 tall with хялгана-style plume tips. The front layer is a low tuft plus 9–19-unit blades and a few thin tall blades, so a crouching brother's eyes (≈G−21) stay visible through the gaps. Blades are seeded by zone, so the layout is stable; wind is two sine waves.
- **Bones (dead world):** pale half-buried long bones, ribcages, sheep/horse skulls (some with ram horns), jaws and fragments, each with a dark outline. Faint cold mist drifts over them. Animal remains only, no gore.
- **Perch:** a bare, leaning dead tree with roots, a broken top stub and side twigs. The top branch spans x−9…x+15 with its top edge at y, so a raven centred on x or starting at x sits on it either way.
- **Safety:** every entry point is wrapped in `save/try/finally restore`, and fields default (hitbox, `anim`, `face`, `state`, `P`). The preview runs 267 calls with junk input and checks that nothing throws and no ctx state leaks.
